# Reports

Reports `Scan / Audit` are designed to be read on two levels.

## Executive summary
The synthesis provides a quick view of exposure, priority issues and already compliant elements.

## Technical details
The details keep the observations, evidence and context useful to the correcting teams.
The report distinguishes between direct findings, deductions and recommendations.

## Sharing
A shared report must remain stable and searchable without restarting the scan.
Sensitive or internal information is not intended for public documentation.
