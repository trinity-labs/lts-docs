# Introduction
`UnyCloud` provides web access to TRINITY files. The service starts from a simple need: making a file space available from the browser, with a clear interface, consistent branding and light enough operation to remain sustainable.

## What UnyCloud is
`UnyCloud` is:

- ✅ A cloud front for files and folders
- ✅ A service described under `<cloud-domain>`
- ✅ A continued continuation of Filebrowser adapted to the TRINITY infrastructure
- ✅ A surface dependent on reverse proxy, mounted storage and branding runtime

## What UnyCloud is not
`UnyCloud` is not:

- ✅ The commercial portal `TRINITY`
- ✅ A remote office like `UnyDesk`
- ✅ An operator console like `UnyPort`
- ✅ A complete post-post synchronization system

## Why this documentation exists
This section documents `UnyCloud` The low-level details Filebrowser remain available in the admin documentation, but the user must be able to understand here the role of the service, its limitations, its access path and its history from Filebrowser to the UnyCloud fork.
