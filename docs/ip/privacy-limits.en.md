# Confidentiality and limitations

`IP` is a diagnostic tool.
It displays the information needed to understand how a connection is viewed from the Internet.

## Confidentiality
- the service does not request an account
- the results displayed are related to the current connection
- the geolocation data are approximate
- browser information may vary depending on the protections enabled

## Limits
GeoIP, ASN, proxy, VPN or Tor databases are never perfect.
A result must be interpreted as a technical index, not as absolute certainty.
